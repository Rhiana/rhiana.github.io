So as a marketer, designer or copy writer what do I need to know about accessibility?

This talk goes over some common terms for accessibiltiy then into more details about how to make:

- Colours that are easy to see
- Words that are easy to read
- Images that are easy to "hear"

Including how to incoporate accessibilty into common tools such as Word, Figma, Canva and Adobe, so any pdfs generated have a higher level of accessibilty baked in.
